Slide 2 · Problem

Finance teams are stitching together customer data, invoices, inbox threads, and memory.

  • 1
    Onboarding is disconnected.
    Customer details, services, and billing intent are often not captured once in a durable way.
  • 2
    Payment evidence lives in email.
    Zelle confirmations arrive in inboxes, not in a structured operations system.
  • 3
    Receipts and follow-up are manual.
    Finance teams re-open threads, re-check amounts, and risk inconsistent customer communication.
The cost is not just time. It is also audit risk, duplicate handling risk, and weak visibility into who has paid, who needs follow-up, and where exceptions are getting stuck.
Business pain today High manual effort + high risk of operational drift
Slide 3 · Solution

Setu Finance creates one operating path from client signup to confirmed receipt.

The product keeps the finance team in control while still automating the expensive and repetitive parts of the work.

Structured client onboarding

Required identity details, optional address, referral source, and dated service enrollments become the finance record from day one.

Invoice flow tied to the customer record

Invoices reuse enrolled services and saved contacts instead of re-keying basic information every time.

Zelle email capture

Inbox sync saves amount, date, memo, transaction number, payer hints, and raw extract into one transaction record.

Human-controlled payment apply

The matching engine prepares the transaction, but finance still decides when to post it into the ledger.

Design principle Automate the preparation, not the financial judgment
Slide 4 · Process Flow

Simple business flow

1

Onboard client

Create the customer record with required contact details, services, referral source, and home address when available.

2

Create invoice

Build the invoice from enrolled services and saved customer data, then send it by email.

3

Sync or save transaction

Bring Zelle confirmation details into the portal as a structured transaction record.

4

Match and queue

Clear matches go to payments to confirm. Mismatches, duplicates, and ambiguity go to exceptions.

5

Apply transaction

A finance user completes the final ledger action with one click after reviewing the prepared record.

6

Completed transactions

The transaction remains visible after apply so finance can send or re-send the receipt later.

7

Send PDF receipt

Receipt goes to the customer’s primary email with amount, transaction number, memo, dates, and invoice reference when available.

8

Update visibility

Dashboard totals, activity history, invoice status, and referral progress update from the applied record.

Simple explanation for business teams Capture → review → apply → communicate

Slide 6 · Controls

Checks and balances are built into the operating model.

  • A
    Saved before applied.
    Every synced transaction is captured as a durable record before finance posts it.
  • B
    Duplicates do not recount.
    Message ID, transaction reference, and invoice-level checks keep the same payment from being counted twice.
  • C
    Exceptions remain actionable.
    Finance can manually match a transaction to an existing customer and move it forward safely.

Audit trail

Activity history shows when a payment was captured, applied, and when the receipt was sent.

Separated actions

Receipt send is a distinct button, which prevents accidental re-posting of money.

Historical integrity

Service enrollments and referral rules are append-only or snapshot-based for reliable backtracking.

Control model Operational speed with explainable financial decisions

Slide 9 · Architecture

Low-cost AWS path without losing product behavior

Frontend

Host the portal on Amazon S3 with CloudFront for low fixed cost and simple HTTPS delivery.

API layer

Move the current Node/Express backend to AWS App Runner for a low-ops managed runtime.

System of record

Use Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL as the managed operational database.

Jobs and sync

Use EventBridge, SQS, and Lambda for scheduled inbox sync, retryable processing, and async email jobs.

Email

Use Amazon SES for lower-cost invoice and receipt sending once the product moves off Gmail SMTP.

Security and monitoring

Keep secrets in Parameter Store or Secrets Manager and use CloudWatch for logs and alarms.

Deployment posture Managed, usage-aware, and ready for broader suite growth
